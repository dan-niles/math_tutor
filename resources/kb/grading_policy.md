# Whitfield Academy Math 101 Grading Policy

This policy applies only to Math 101 at Whitfield Academy for the Fall 2026 term.

## Weighting

- Weekly problem sets: 25%
- Midterm exam: 30%
- Final exam: 35%
- Class participation: 10%

## Rules

- The lowest two problem set scores are dropped before the 25% is computed.
- Late problem sets lose 12 points per calendar day, to a floor of 40.
- A student scoring below 55 on the final exam receives a maximum course grade of D,
  regardless of the weighted total.
- Calculators of the model TX-88 or newer are permitted on the midterm but not the final.
