# Order of Operations (PEMDAS)

Operations in an arithmetic expression are evaluated in this order:

1. **P**arentheses
2. **E**xponents
3. **M**ultiplication and **D**ivision, left to right
4. **A**ddition and **S**ubtraction, left to right

Multiplication does not outrank division; whichever appears first from the left is
evaluated first. The same applies to addition and subtraction.

## Worked Example

Evaluate `2 x 7 - 9 / 3 + 8`.

1. Multiply: 2 x 7 = 14
2. Divide: 9 / 3 = 3
3. Subtract: 14 - 3 = 11
4. Add: 11 + 8 = 19

The answer is 19.
